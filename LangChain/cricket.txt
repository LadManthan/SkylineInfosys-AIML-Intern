CRICKET

Cricket is unique for having three distinct international formats, each demanding a different set of skills and mindset.
Test Cricket: The ultimate "marathon." Played over 5 days with unlimited overs, it tests a player’s endurance and technique.
ODI : A 50 over format which strikes a balance between the patient strategy of Tests and the explosive power of T20s.
T20 : With only 20 overs per side, it’s high-octane, filled with big hits, and usually finishes in under 4 hours.

ICONS OF THE GAME

Virat Kohli (The Run Machine)
The "King" of modern batting. Known for his intense fitness and unmatched ability to chase down totals.

Rohit Sharma (The Hitman)
The master of "effortless power." Rohit is the only player in history with three double-centuries in ODIs.
